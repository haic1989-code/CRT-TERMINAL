## Review notes

- The read-only MT5 bridge emits transport payloads. `SmartFlowShell` maps them to `AccountSnapshot`, `Position`, `PendingOrder` and `SymbolSpec` before engine calls.
- `MarketProfileEngine` reports `bar_range_tpo`; it does not claim access to exchange-traded volume.
- Risk Guard has veto priority. Missing/stale account or quote data and incomplete stop exposure cannot produce an allowed result.
- The implementation is not an MQL5 source-code port. If a future change copies or ports upstream code, that change must add the applicable copyright/license text to `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` before merge.
